Source

Direct COPC streams the source. 3D Tiles chooses certified bounds or a matching saved index automatically; if a full point scan is needed, you will review its cost before it starts.

The current source stays active while the selected source is checked and loaded.

Advanced 3D Tiles controls

An approved scan derives its minimum point budget from the complete hierarchy (up to 20 million points). Independent limits remain: 4,096 hierarchy nodes, 256 MiB source point data, 384 MiB decoded ECEF output, 2 million points per node, and 16 MiB per GLB. Metadata is read before approval; no full point scan starts silently.
